AI Girl Studio · Behind the scenes

Four accounts, one computer, no subscription

Every picture, every video and every sound on these accounts is made on a single home computer, with open models anyone can download. Here is how — in plain words.

01Each account runs on recipes

An account is not one prompt typed over and over. It has a dozen or more recipes: a small scene with its rules — a summer dress caught by the wind, a mirror that does not behave, a surprise kiss, a walk that turns strange.

For each post, the machine picks a recipe and writes everything at once: the caption, the description of the first picture, and what happens in the video. Recipes take turns, and the ones people watch the most come back more often.

What makes people stay: the first three seconds of the video, not the caption or the hashtags. The first picture has to promise something — the viewer stays to see it happen.

02From a recipe to a post

  1. The words — caption and descriptions, written by a language model running on the same computer.
  2. The first picture — and sometimes the last one too, so the video knows where it ends.
  3. The video — 15 seconds, animated from that picture, with its own sound.
  4. Or a carousel — several pictures, the first one gently animated.
  5. A human says yes — nothing goes out without a person looking at it, then it is scheduled.

03The models we use

Two image models, chosen per recipe because they do not draw the same girls:

Krea 2 Turbo Fast, and keeps the same face from one picture to the next in a carousel.
MOSS-Image More varied faces, and stays photographic even in impossible places.

And for movement:

MiniMax H3 Turns the first picture into a 15-second video with its own sound — voices, music, ambience.
Wan 2.2 Animates the first slide of a carousel.

Everything runs in ComfyUI. The models, and the add-ons (LoRAs) that give each account its look, are shared by the community on Hugging Face and CivitAI. Each one has its own licence — read it before using it, especially for anything you earn money with.

04One computer, overnight

All of it runs on one home computer with a gaming graphics card (12 GB of video memory). No monthly AI subscription, and no online service sees the pictures before they are posted. A video takes several minutes, so most of the work happens at night; during the day, a person only chooses what goes out.

05The honest part

  • Every post is labelled as AI. None of these girls is a real person, and none is made to look like one.
  • Each platform has its own rules on suggestive content — they apply, AI or not.
  • It is not passive income. It is a craft: the machine does the hours, a person still decides.
